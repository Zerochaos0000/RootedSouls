    Frequently asked questions

    What do homes at Blackston Cove cost?

    Pre-construction prices start at $1,299,000 for the Concord II and run to $1,510,300 for the five-bedroom Conestoga. Homesite premiums range from $0 to $75,000. See pricing for every plan.

    How big are the homesites?

    Each of the 14 homes sits on its own homesite of about one acre, within a 43-acre community off Summit Bridge Road, two miles from Middletown.

    Can I personalize my home?

    Yes. Start from one of our suggested designs and modify it, or bring your own custom home plans. The Hellings selections team will help you choose finishes and options.

    View the included features list (PDF)
    What is the Hellings building process?

    Consultation and feasibility, initial design, final design and pre-construction, then construction managed by a Hellings project manager. Read more about the builder.

    I still own a home. Can I buy now?

    Yes. Many buyers sell their current home while their new one is being built. These two short videos explain how, or talk with the sales team about your situation.
